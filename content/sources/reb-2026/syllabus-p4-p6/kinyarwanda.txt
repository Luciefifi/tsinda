IJAMBO RY'IBANZE

Urwego rw'Igihugu rushinzwe Uburezi bw'Ibanze (REB) rushimishijwe no kubagezaho integanyanyigisho
y'Ikinyarwanda, ikiciro cya kabiri cy'amashuri abanza, icapwa rya kabiri. Iyi nteganyanyigisho igamije gutanga
uburezi buboneye kandi bufite ireme mu mashuri yose y'u Rwanda. Iyi nteganyanyigisho yateguwe n'abahanga mu
Kinyarwanda , abarimu ndetse n'abategura integanyanyigisho ni urufunguzo rwo guteza imbere Integanyanyigisho
Ishingiye ku Bushobozi (CBC) yerekana Poritiki y'uburezi mu Rwanda yo kurera abanyeshuri bafite ubumenyi,
ubumenyi ngiro, ubukesha ndetse n'imyitwarire myiza.

Uburezi bw'ibanze bufite ireme ni umusingi w'iterambere n'ubukungu birambye by'u Rwanda. Dushingiye kuri poritiki
y'Igihugu mu burezi, iyi nteganyanyigisho yibanda ku gutekereza byimbitse, guhanga udushya, uburezi budaheza
no guteza imbere umunyeshuri mu buryo busesuye. Igamije guha abanyeshuri ubushobozi bukenewe kugira ngo
babashe kugera kure hashoboka, bagire ubumenyi bukenewe, ubumenyi-ngiro, ubukesha n'indangagaciro bikwiye.
Ibyo bizatuma bagira ubushobozi bwo gupigana ku isoko ry'umurimo haba mu karere u Rwanda ruherereyemo
cyangwa ku rwego mpuzamahanga.

Dushingiye ku kerekezo cy'u Rwanda 2050 ndetse na Gahunda y'Igihugu yo Kwihutisha Iterambere (NST2), iyi
nteganyanyigisho ishyigikira intego y'Igihugu yo kubaka ubukungu bushingiye ku bumenyi, ku guhanga udushya, ku
gusigasira ibyagezweho no gupiganwa ku rwego mpuzamahanga. Ishyira imbere uburyo bwo kwigisha bushingiye
ku munyeshuri, uburyo buha umwanya umunyeshuri no gushyira mu bikorwa ubumenyi yahawe, bimutegurira
gukomeza amashuri no kuba umuturage uzi inshingano ze, ugira imyitwarire myiza kandi ushobora gutanga
umusanzu mu mpinduramatwara zikomeje mu Rwanda.

Iyi nteganyanyigisho igaragaza ubushake dufite bwo guha abanyeshuri ubumenyi n'ubushobozi bikenewe mu
kinyejana cya 21, bushyira imbere udushya no gutoza abanyeshuri indangagaciro zirimo ubunyangamugayo, kwita
ku mibereho y'abandi no guhora biyungura ubumenyi. Gutsinda kw'abanyeshuri gushingira cyane ku kwitangira

GUSHIMIRA

Itegurwa ry'iyi nteganyanyigisho ni umusaruro w'ubufatanye, ubwitange, ubumenyi n'ubushake
bw'abafatanyabikorwa batandukanye. Mu izina ry'Ishami ry'Integanyanyigisho n'Imfashanyigisho mu Rwego
rw'Igihugu rushinzwe Uburezi bw'Ibanze (REB), ndashimira byimazeyo buri wese wagize uruhare mu gutegura
neza iyi nteganyanyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda mu kiciro cya kabiri cy'amashuri abanza (P4­P6).

Ndashimira by'umwihariko, abahanga mu isomo ry'Ikinyarwanda, abategura integanyanyigisho, abarimu
ndetse n'abafatanyabikorwa mu burezi batandukanye, bagize uruhare rukomeye mu gutanga ibitekerezo, inama
n'ubuhanga bagaragaje mu kunoza iyi nteganyanyigisho. Ubufatanye bwabo bwatumye iyi nteganyanyigisho
ihuzwa n'intego z'integanyanyigisho y'u Rwanda Ishingiye ku Bushobozi (CBC) kandi isubiza ibyifuzo by'ikerekezo
2050 n'ibya Gahunda y'Igihugu yo Kwihutisha Iterambere (NST2).

Turashimira kandi inkunga ikomeye y'abafatanyabikorwa mu iterambere n'abandi bafatanyabikorwa mu burezi,
badufashije mu buryo bwa tekiniki mu guhuza no kunoza iyi nteganyanyigisho. Ubufatanye bwanyu bwagize
uruhare rukomeye mu kongera imbaraga no guteza imbere ireme ry'uburezi mu Rwanda.

Mu gusoza, turasaba abarimu, abayobozi b'amashuri n'abandi bose bakora mu burezi gukoresha iyi
nteganyanyigisho kinyamwuga kandi bitanga kugira ngo ishuri rihinduke ahantu hatangirwa ubumenyi, ubumenyi-
ngiro, indangagaciro n'imyitwarire myiza ku banyeshuri. Kubera ubwitange bwanyu, iyi nteganyanyigisho izagera
ku ntego yayo yo gufasha abanyeshuri kugera ku bushobozi bukenewe no kugira uruhare rukomeye mu iterambere
rirambye ry'u Rwanda.

1. INTANGIRIRO RUSANGE

1.1. Amavu n'amavuko y'ivugururwa ry'iteganyanyigisho

Ivugururwa ry'iteganyanyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda mu kiciro cya kabiri cy'amashuri abanza rigaragaza ubushake
u Rwanda rufite bwo gushimangira Integanyanyigisho Ishingiye ku Bushobozi (CBC), yatangiye gushyirwa mu
bikorwa mu mwaka wa 2015. Ibitekerezo bitandukanye byatanzwe n'abarimu, abayobozi b'amashuri ndetse
n'abafatanyabikorwa mu burezi, byagaragaje ko hakenewe kunoza uburyo bwo kwigisha, igihe kigenerwa amasomo
no kwita ku byo abanyeshuri bakeneye.

Iyi nteganyanyigisho, icapwa rya kabiri, ishyira mu bikorwa amabwiriza ya Minisiteri arebana no guhuza amasaha
y'ishuri akagirwa atanu ku munsi n'ibyumweru 33 by'amasomo ku mwaka. Ibi bigamije guhuza integanyanyigisho
n'ikerekezo 2050 na Gahunda y'Igihugu yo Kwihutisha Iterambere (NST2), byibanda ku bukungu bushingiye ku
bumenyi, ku baturage barangwa n'indangagaciro ndetse no ku iterambere rirambye.

Integanyanyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda yubakiye ku nsanganyamatsiko z'inyuranye nk'umuco nyarwanda, gukunda
Igihugu, ubumwe, kubana neza n'abandi, isuku, imikino n'imyidagaduro, ibidukikije n'izindi, byose bigamije
kugaragaza ubudasa bw'umuco nyarwanda mu ruhando rw'amahanga.

Binyuze mu masomo ari muri iyi nteganyanyigisho, abanyeshuri bazunguka ubumenyi, ubumenyi-ngiro, ubukesha
n'indangagaciro bibategurira guhangana n'ibibazo by'ubuzima bwa buri munsi, gufata ibyemezo byiza no gukura
ari abaturage beza kandi batanga umusaruro.

2. KWIGISHA NO KWIGA IKINYARWANDA

2.1. Impamvu zo kwigisha no kwiga Ikinyarwanda

2.1.1. Ikinyarwanda nk'ururimi kavukire

Ikinyarwanda ni ururimi kavukire ruhuza Abanyarwanda bose kuko rukoreshwa mu nzego zose z'ubutegetsi
n'iz'imirimo, rwumvwa kandi rukanavugwa n'Abanyarwanda hafi ya bose. Ikinyarwanda kigishwa mu mashuri
nk'inyigisho kandi kigakoreshwa mu kwigisha izindi nyigisho mu kiciro cy'amashuri y'inshuke. Ikindi kandi
Ikinyarwanda kibumbatiye umuco w'Igihugu, gituma dushyikirana, tukungurana ibitekerezo, tukagezanyaho
ubutumwa, tukagaragaza n'imbamutima zacu.

Nk'uko abahanga mu byo kwigisha babivuga, kumenya ururimi kavukire ni intango ndetse n'imbarutso yo kumenya
izindi ndimi. Abarimu barakangurirwa rero guha agaciro Ikinyarwanda kuko ari uguha agaciro imyigishirize
ndetse no gufasha mu kuzamura ireme ry'uburezi. Ururimi rwacu ruri mu bigaragaza imitekerereze, ubuhanga
n'umuco byacu, rukabicengeza mu rubyiruko, bikaba uruhererekane, maze ntitumirwe n'amahanga. Ururimi
rw'Ikinyarwanda rufite uruhare rukomeye mu guhamya Umunyarwanda udafite isoni n'ubwoba by'umuco we
kandi utisuzugura. Umuco w'u Rwanda ugomba gusigasirwa no gukungahazwa. Inzira ya mbere yo kuwusigasira
ni ukwigisha Ikinyarwanda, cyanecyane ko gikoreshwa hamwe n'izindi ndimi z'amahanga zishobora kuwumira.
Ikinyarwanda rero ni rwo rurimi rukoreshwa mu kwigisha gusoma no kwandika by'ibanze bityo kikaba kigomba
kwigwa no kwigishwa ku buryo buboneye kugira ngo byoroshye n'imyigire y'izindi ndimi.

2.1.2. Ibirebana n'umunyeshuri

Mu kwiga no kwigisha Ikinyarwanda hazibandwa ku bushobozi bwo kuvuga, kumva, gusoma, kwandika ndetse
n'ubumenyi bw'ururimi. Icyakora mu kwiga no kwigisha Ikinyarwanda ni ngombwa guhuriza hamwe buriya
bushobozi bwose. Nubwo umunyeshuri wo mu kiciro cya kabiri yumva kandi akavuga Ikinyarwanda, imvugo ye iba
ikeneye gukosorwa buhorobuhoro ndetse no gukungahazwa kugira ngo akure arushaho kwisanzura mu rurimi rwe.

Nubwo abanyeshuri bo mu kiciro cya kabari baba bazi gusoma no kwandika, basabwa gukora imyitozo inyuranye
yo kubihamya kandi bakitoza gusesengura no guhimba imyandiko bahereye ku ngero bize. Umwarimu yerekera
abanyeshuri, akabayobora, akabatera inkunga, akabakosora kugira ngo batunganye interuro zabo, batore
kwitegereza, kwigana, isuku no kunoza umukono.

Inyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda ifatira ku mwandiko. Umwandiko ugomba kuba ari intangarugero ku miterere yawo no
ku ngingo ziwurimo. Umwandiko umwe ushobora gukoreshwaho imyitozo yo gusoma, kwiyungura amagambo,
gusesengura, gutahura ingingo z'ingenzi n'ubumenyi bw'indangamuco kimwe n'imihimbire igendeye kuri buri
bwoko bw'umwandiko.

2.2. Ubushobozi

Ubushobozi ni ububasha bwo gukora umurimo uyu n'uyu wihariye uko bikwiye hashingiwe ku bumenyi,
ubumenyi ngiro n'ubukesha bijyanye n'ibyo wize. Poritiki z'Igihugu zishingiye ku byo Igihugu gikeneye zerekana
ubushobozi bw'ibanze n'ubushobozi nsanganyamasomo bishingirwaho mu kubaka imitekerereze yagutse. Muri
iyi nteganyanyigisho ubushobozi bw'ibanze bugaragarira mu bushobozi bugamijwe muri buri mutwe, muri buri
mwaka ndetse no mu kiciro. Ibikorwa by'umunyeshuri bigomba gushingira ku byo ashobora gukora kugira ngo
hatezwe imbere ubushobozi bugamijwe mu gihe yiga cyangwa arangije umutwe w'ibyigwa runaka.

2.2.1. Ubushobozi nsanganyamasomo

Ubushobozi nsanganyamasomo ni ubushobozi butihariwe n'inyigisho imwe; ahubwo butezwa imbere mu masomo
anyuranye, mu bihe bitandukanye ndetse no mu kazi kanyuranye. Ubushobozi bw'ingenzi mu myigire n'imyigishirize
abarimu bagomba kwitaho busobanuye ku buryo bukurikira:

-Ubushishozi no gushakira ibibazo ibisubizo: Ubu bushobozi buzafasha umunyeshuri gutekereza ahanga no
 gushyira mu gaciro ku buryo bwaguye bituma abonera ibisubizo ibibazo ahura na byo mu buzima bwa buri munsi.

-Guhanga udushya: Kunguka ubu bushobozi bizafasha umunyeshuri gufata iya mbere agatekereza ahereye ku
 byo azi, maze agahanga ibintu bishya.

-Ubushakashatsi: Ubu bushobozi buzafasha umunyeshuri gukemura ibibazo ahereye ku makuru no ku bumenyi
 busanzweho no gusobanura ibintu ahereye ku makuru yakusanyije.

-Gusabana mu ndimi zemewe gukoreshwa mu Gihugu: Ubu bushobozi buzafasha abanyeshuri gusabana n'abandi
 nta mususu no gutanga ibitekerezo byabo ku buryo buboneye mu mvugo no mu nyandiko bakoresha imvugo
 n'amagambo bikwiye. Kubera iyo mpamvu, abarimu bose nubwo baba batigisha indimi basabwa kugenzura ko
 abanyeshuri bakoresha uko bikwiye ururimi rwigishwamo.

-Ubufatanye, imibanire ikwiye n'abandi n'ubumenyi ngiro mu buzima bwa buri munsi: Ubu bushobozi
 buzafasha abanyeshuri gukorana n'abandi mu matsinda, mu kazi ako ari ko kose bahawe barangwa n'imyitwarire
 n'indangagaciro bikwiye, bubaha uburenganzira, ibitekerezo n'imyitwarire binyuranye n'ibyabo. Ibi bizafasha
 kandi abanyeshuri gukora ibikorwa bijyanye no kubungabunga ibidukikije, gukora ubuvugizi ku byerekeye
 ubuzima bwabo n'ubw'umuryango bita ku isuku n'imirire iboneye kandi nanone bakemura ibibazo bahura na byo
 mu buzima.

-Kwiga no guhora yiyungura ubumenyi: kunguka ubu bushobozi bizafasha abanyeshuri kujyana n'igihe biyungura
 ubumenyi n'ubumenyi ngiro batarinze gufashwa muri byose. Ibi bizabafasha kandi kujyana n'iterambere bibanda
 ku bumenyi n'ubumenyi ngiro bikenewe.

2.2.2. Ubushobozi rusange bugamijwe mu nyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda

Integanyanyigisho y'ikiciro cya kabiri cy'amashuri abanza igomba kugeza umunyeshuri ku bushobozi bukurikira:

-Kumara umwanya yumva ibitekerezo abwirwa cyangwa asomerwa, cyangwa akurikiranye ubutumwa bunyuzwa
 mu mafirime no mu bitangazamakuru binyuranye;

2.2.3. Ikinyarwanda no kwimakaza ubushobozi bugamijwe

Poritiki y'Igihugu mu myigishirize y'Ikinyarwanda ni ugutoza umunyeshuri w'Umunyarwanda umuco wo gusoma
akiri muto ndetse no guhanga ahereye ku ngero z'imyandiko y'ubuvanganzo yigishijwe. Usibye ubushobozi bwo
kuvuga, kumva, gusoma no kwandika neza Ikinyarwanda, umunyeshuri ahabwa ubushobozi n'ubumenyi ngiro
rusange bimufasha kwibeshaho no kubana neza n'abandi.

Muri iyi nteganyanyigisho nshya twitaye kandi ku bumenyi ngiro ku buryo bw'umwihariko kugira ngo dufashe
abana b'u Rwanda mu buzima bwabo bwa buri munsi kandi tubashishikarize kwita ku bibazo bibangamiye isi muri
rusange n'Igihugu cyacu ku buryo bw'umwihariko. Ingingo zitaweho cyanecyane ni umuco nyarwanda, isuku,
umuryango, ibidukikije, uburenganzira bw'umwana, kwirinda no gukumira ihohoterwa, gukunda Igihugu, umuco
w'amahoro, inyamaswa zo mu rugo, indyo yuzuye, kuzigama, inshingano z'abana, imiyoborere myiza, itumanaho

4. UBURYO BWO GUKORA ISUZUMA

Isuzuma ni ikurikirana ry'imyigire y'umunyeshuri hakusanywa ibimenyetso bijyanye n'uburyo buri munyeshuri
yiga ndetse no gufata umwanzuro ku byo umunyeshuri yagezeho hashingiwe ku bipimo byagenwe mbere yo gukora

isuzuma na ryo rigomba gushingira ku bushobozi, ahoumunyeshuri ashobora gukora umwitozo ujyanye n'ubuzima
bwa buri munsi ashyira mu bikorwa ibyo yize.

abanyeshuri basanganywe. Iri suzuma rikorwa ku ntangiriro y'isomo kugira ngo riyobore imyigishirize.

ingingo ngenderwaho mu gusuzuma urwego rw'ubushobozi (ubumenyi, ubumenyi ngiro n'ubukesha) abanyeshuri

umunyeshuri akora ijyanye n'imyigire ye. Ibyavuye mu isuzuma ni byo bizagaragaza ko umunyeshuri yakurikiye

5. IBIKORESHO

Kugira ngo integanyanyigisho y'ikinyarwanda ishyirwe mu bikorwa hakenewe ibikoresho bihagije birimo ibitabo
n'izindi mfashanyigisho kuko kutabigira byatuma hataboneka umusaruro ukwiye. Hakenewe kandi abarimu
bashoboye kwigisha isomo ry'Ikinyarwanda.

5.1. Ibikoresho bizakenerwa mu ishyirwa mu bikorwa ry'iyi nteganyanyigisho

Hariho ubwoko butandukanye bw'ibikoresho bukenewe kugira ngo habeho uburyo bwiza bwo kwigisha no kwiga
isomo ry'Ikinyarwanda. Ibyo bikoresho biha abanyeshuri amahirwe yo gushyira mu bikorwa ibyo bize, gutekereza
byimbitse kandi bibafasha kugira ubushobozi bukwiye. Ibyo bikoresho ni ibi bikurikira:

-Ibitabo byo kwigiramo n'ibitabo by'inyongera: Ibitabo byo kwigiramo by'abanyeshuri, ibitabo byo gusoma,
 ibitabo by'umwarimu n'izindi mfashanyigisho bashobora kwifashisha mu gihe bigisha.

-Ibikoresho bifasha mu kwigisha no kwiga: Ibikoresho bifatika bifasha mu kwigisha nk' amakarita, amashusho,
 videwo, radiyo, imbuga z'uburezi, kwiga hakoreshejwe murandasi n'ibindi bikoresho by'ikoranabuhanga byafasha
 mu kwigisha no kwiga.

-Ibikoresho byo mu ishuri: Amakarita ariho inyuguti, impapuro nini ziriho inyuguti, ibimenyetso by'amarangi,
 n'ibikoresho by'ibanze byo kwandika bifasha mu masomo ya buri munsi mu ishuri.

-Ibikoresho byo mu isomero n'ahandi habikwa ibikoresho: Kugira isomero ryuzuye rifite ibitabo byiyongera ku
 masomo, ibinyamakuru n'inyandiko z'inyunganizi ku muco n'indangagaciro.

-Ibikoresho byo gusura ahantu: Ibikoresho bifasha mu ngendo z'uburezi nk'inyandiko, ibitabo byo kwandikamo
 n'ibindi. Gusura ahantu no guhura n'abaturage ni ingenzi mu kwigisha no mu kwiga.

Abarimu bagomba kwerekana ubunyangamugayo, kwihangana, kubaha abandi no kwita ku nshingano no kugira

n'aho batuye.

6. IMITWE Y'IYI NTEGANYANYIGISHO

6.1. Imiterere y'imitwe muri iyi nteganyanyigisho

Inyigisho y'Ikinyarwanda yigwa mu kiciro cya mbere cy'amashuri abanza nk'isomo. Ibyigwa mu kiciro cya mbere
cy'amashuri abanza bikubiye mu mitwe cumi n'umunani (18). Ni ukuvuga ko buri mwaka ufite imitwe itandatu.
Umutwe ushingiye ku nsanganyamatsiko iherwaho kugira ngo abanyeshuri bashobore gusoma, gusesengura no
guhanga imyandiko ndetse no gukungahaza ubumenyi bwabo mu rurimi basesengura ikibonezamvugo.

Integanyanyigisho ya buri mwaka igizwe n'imbumbanyigisho zigabanyijwemo inyigisho zinyuranye naho inyigisho
ikaba igabanyijwemo imitwe inyuranye. Buri mutwe ugira ubushobozi bw'ingenzi bugamijwe kugerwaho mu
gihe umaze kwigwa wose. Kugira ngo ubwo bushobozi bugerweho, hari intego z'ubumenyi, iz'ubumenyi ngiro
n'iz'ubukesha zifasha umwarimu mu gutegura no gutanga isomo rye ashingiye ku byigwa binyuranye bigizwe
n'ikibonezamvugo.

Intego zijyanye n'ubumenyi, umuntu yavuga ko ari zo ziri ku rwego rw'ibanze. Intego zijyanye n'ubumenyi ngiro
ndetse n'ubukesha, umuntu yavuga ko ziri ku rwego rwisumbuye. Uru rwego rw'intego ni rwo rwibanzweho cyane

Ingingo nsaranganyamasomo

Muri uyu mutwe hibandwa cyane ku ngingo nsanganyamasomo yo kubungabunga ibidukikije aho bigaragarira
mu myandiko itandukanye yerekana ibyiza bitatse u Rwanda.

mvugo no mu nyandiko.
