# Dividing a whole number by a fraction

## What you will learn

You will divide a whole number, like 32, by a fraction, like 3/5.

## Think first

You have 4 chocolate bars. You give each friend half a bar.

- How many friends get a piece?
- So what is 4 ÷ 1/2?

## How to do it

**Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.**

1. Change ÷ into ×.
2. Turn the fraction after the ÷ upside down (its reciprocal).
3. Multiply, then simplify.

If the fraction is a mixed number, change it to an improper fraction first.

## Example 1

Divide 32 by 3/5.

- 32 ÷ 3/5 = 32 × 5/3
- = 160/3
- = **53 1/3**

## Example 2

Divide 38 by 1 8/11.

- Change the mixed number: 1 8/11 = 19/11
- 38 ÷ 19/11 = 38 × 11/19
- 38 and 19 can be simplified: 38 ÷ 19 = 2
- = 2 × 11 = **22**

## Remember

÷ a fraction = × its reciprocal. "Keep, change, flip": keep the first number, change ÷ to ×, flip the fraction.
