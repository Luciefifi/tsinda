# Word problems with fractions

## What you will learn

You will solve real-life problems that use fractions: sharing land, money, votes or food.

## Think first

Mbabazi and Rugari wanted to be chairperson of their savings group. Mbabazi got 1/3 of the votes and Rugari got the rest. 3,600 people voted.

- How many people voted for Mbabazi?
- What fraction of the votes did Rugari get? How many people is that?

## How to solve a word problem

1. **Read** the problem slowly, two times.
2. **Find** what you know and what you must find.
3. **Choose** the operation: × (often the word "of") or ÷.
4. **Work it out**, step by step. Show your working: in the exam you get marks for the steps.
5. **Check** that your answer makes sense, and write the unit (ha, Frw, km...).

**Useful idea:** the whole thing (all the land, all the money) is **1**. If someone uses 1/4, what is left is 1 − 1/4 = 3/4.

## Example 1

Uwera gave 2/5 of her land to her son Kwizera and 3/8 of the land to her daughter Cissy.
a) What fraction of the land did she keep?
b) She kept 18 hectares. How much land did she have at first?

**a)** The whole land is 1.
- 1 − 2/5 − 3/8 = 40/40 − 16/40 − 15/40 = **9/40**

**b)** 9/40 of the land is 18 ha.
- Whole land = 18 ÷ 9/40 = 18 × 40/9 = **80 ha**

## Example 2

Moses spent 1/4 of his money on food. He spent 1/3 of the money that was left on transport. He still had 12,000 Frw. How much money did he have at first?

- After food: 1 − 1/4 = 3/4 is left.
- Transport: 1/3 of 3/4 = 1/4.
- Left after transport: 3/4 − 1/4 = 2/4 = 1/2.
- 1/2 of his money is 12,000 Frw, so all his money is 12,000 × 2 = **24,000 Frw**.

## Remember

Read carefully, decide × or ÷, show every step, and write the unit in your answer.
