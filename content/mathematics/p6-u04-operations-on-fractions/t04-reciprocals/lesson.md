# Finding reciprocals

## What you will learn

You will find the **reciprocal** of a number. You need reciprocals to divide fractions.

## Think first

5 × ? = 1

- What number makes this true?

## What is a reciprocal?

The reciprocal of a number is the number you multiply it by to get **1**.

## How to find it

- **For a fraction:** turn it upside down. The reciprocal of a/b is b/a.
- **For a whole number:** write it as a fraction first. 5 = 5/1, so its reciprocal is 1/5.
- **For a mixed number:** change it to an improper fraction first. 2 3/4 = 11/4, so its reciprocal is 4/11.

## Examples

1. The reciprocal of 5 is **1/5**, because 5 × 1/5 = 5/5 = 1.
2. The reciprocal of 1/3 is **3**, because 1/3 × 3 = 3/3 = 1.
3. The reciprocal of 3/4 is **4/3**, because 3/4 × 4/3 = 12/12 = 1.

## Remember

Turn the fraction upside down. A number times its reciprocal is always 1. (0 has no reciprocal.)
