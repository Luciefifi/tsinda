# Dividing a fraction by a whole number

## What you will learn

You will divide a fraction, like 1/2, by a whole number, like 3.

## Think first

Cut an orange into 2 halves. Then share one half equally between 3 friends.

- Does each friend get more or less than a half?
- What fraction of the whole orange does each friend get?

## How to do it

1. Write the fraction.
2. Change ÷ into ×.
3. Use the reciprocal of the whole number: the reciprocal of 3 is 1/3.
4. Multiply, then simplify.

## Example 1

Divide 1/2 by 3.

- The reciprocal of 3 is 1/3.
- 1/2 ÷ 3 = 1/2 × 1/3 = **1/6**

## Example 2

Work out: 3 1/4 ÷ 13

- Change the mixed number: 3 1/4 = 13/4
- 13/4 ÷ 13 = 13/4 × 1/13
- 13 and 13 cancel out.
- = **1/4**

## Remember

Dividing by a whole number makes the fraction smaller. ÷ 3 is the same as × 1/3.
