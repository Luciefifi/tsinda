# Multiplying a whole number by a fraction

## What you will learn

You will multiply a whole number, like 15, by a fraction, like 2/3.

## Think first

A loaf of bread has 8 slices. You give 1/8 of the bread to each child.

- How many slices do 5 children get together?
- What fraction of the bread is that?

## How to do it

1. Multiply the whole number by the **numerator** (the top number).
2. Divide the answer by the **denominator** (the bottom number).
3. Simplify if you can.

**Tip:** You can write any whole number as a fraction by putting 1 under it. For example, 15 = 15/1.

## Example

Multiply: 15 × 2/3

- Write 1 under 15: 15 × 2/3 = 15/1 × 2/3
- Multiply the top numbers: 15 × 2 = 30
- Multiply the bottom numbers: 1 × 3 = 3
- So the answer is 30/3 = **10**

## Remember

Whole number × fraction: multiply by the top number, then divide by the bottom number.
