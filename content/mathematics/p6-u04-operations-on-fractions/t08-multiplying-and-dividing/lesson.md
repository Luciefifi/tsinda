# Multiplying and dividing fractions together

## What you will learn

You will work out calculations that have both × and ÷, like 1/2 × 2/3 ÷ 5/6.

## Think first

Write three fractions. Multiply the first two. Then divide your answer by the third fraction.

- Which steps did you follow?

## How to do it

There are two ways. Both give the same answer.

**Method 1: division first (BODMAS)**
1. Work out the division first.
2. Then multiply.
3. Simplify.

**Method 2: all in one line**
1. Change every mixed number to an improper fraction.
2. Change every ÷ into × and flip the fraction that comes after it.
3. Multiply everything, then simplify.

## Example

Work out: 1/2 × 2/3 ÷ 5/6

**Method 1**
- Division first: 2/3 ÷ 5/6 = 2/3 × 6/5 = 12/15
- Then multiply: 1/2 × 12/15 = 12/30 = **2/5**

**Method 2**
- 1/2 × 2/3 × 6/5 = 12/30 = **2/5**

## Example 2

Work out: 1 1/3 ÷ 3/4 × 2 1/2

- Improper fractions: 1 1/3 = 4/3 and 2 1/2 = 5/2
- Flip the fraction after ÷: 4/3 × 4/3 × 5/2
- = 80/18 = 40/9 = **4 4/9**

## Remember

Change mixed numbers first. Then flip the fraction after every ÷ and multiply everything.
