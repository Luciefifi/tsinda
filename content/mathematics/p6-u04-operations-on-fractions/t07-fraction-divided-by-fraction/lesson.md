# Dividing a fraction by a fraction

## What you will learn

You will divide one fraction by another, like 3/4 ÷ 1/3.

## Think first

Draw a circle and cut it into 8 equal parts. Shade 3 parts. Now cut the shaded part into 2 equal pieces and shade one piece again.

- What fraction of the whole circle is shaded twice?

## How to do it

1. Change any mixed numbers into improper fractions.
2. Keep the first fraction.
3. Change ÷ into ×.
4. Flip the second fraction (use its reciprocal).
5. Multiply, then simplify.

## Example 1

Work out: 3/4 ÷ 1/3

- The reciprocal of 1/3 is 3/1.
- 3/4 ÷ 1/3 = 3/4 × 3/1 = 9/4 = **2 1/4**

## Example 2

Divide: 4 2/3 ÷ 1 3/4

- Change to improper fractions: 4 2/3 = 14/3 and 1 3/4 = 7/4
- 14/3 ÷ 7/4 = 14/3 × 4/7
- 14 and 7 simplify: 14 ÷ 7 = 2, so we get 2/3 × 4/1 = 8/3
- = **2 2/3**

## Remember

Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, flip the second fraction.
