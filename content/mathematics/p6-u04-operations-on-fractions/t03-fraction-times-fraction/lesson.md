# Multiplying a fraction by a fraction

## What you will learn

You will multiply two fractions, like 5/6 × 3/4.

## Think first

Draw a square and divide it into 10 equal strips. Shade 5 strips. Now cut the square in half with a line across, and shade half of the square in another colour.

- How many small parts are shaded twice?
- Now work out 1/2 × 5/10. Is it the same?

## How to do it

1. Multiply the **numerators** (top numbers) together.
2. Multiply the **denominators** (bottom numbers) together.
3. Simplify.

In short: a/b × c/d = (a × c) / (b × d)

**If there is a mixed number** like 2 1/4, change it to an improper fraction first: 2 1/4 = 9/4.

## Example

Simplify: 5/6 × 3/4

- Top: 5 × 3 = 15
- Bottom: 6 × 4 = 24
- 15/24 = **5/8** (divide top and bottom by 3)

## Remember

Top × top, bottom × bottom, then simplify.
