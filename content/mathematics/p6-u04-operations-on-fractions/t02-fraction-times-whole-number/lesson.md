# Multiplying a fraction by a whole number

## What you will learn

You will multiply a fraction, like 5/12, by a whole number, like 24. You will also learn that "of" means "multiply".

## Think first

A sugarcane is cut into equal parts.

- If it has 4 parts, what fraction is one part?
- Multiply that fraction by 4. What do you get?

## How to do it

1. Multiply the **numerator** (top number) by the whole number.
2. Keep the **denominator** (bottom number).
3. Simplify the fraction.

**The word "of" means multiply.** 3/4 of 20 is the same as 3/4 × 20.

## Example 1

Multiply: 5/12 × 24

- Write 1 under 24: 5/12 × 24/1
- Top: 5 × 24 = 120. Bottom: 12 × 1 = 12
- 120/12 = **10**

## Example 2

What is 3/4 of 20?

- 3/4 of 20 = 3/4 × 20 = (3 × 20) / 4 = 60/4 = **15**

## Remember

Fraction × whole number: multiply the top number by the whole number and keep the bottom number. "Of" means ×.
