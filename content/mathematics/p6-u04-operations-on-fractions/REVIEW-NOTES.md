# Review notes: P6 Mathematics, Unit 4 (Operations on fractions)

For the reviewer. Everything in this unit is a draft until you approve it.

Source: P6 Mathematics Pupil's Book (REB, 2026–2027 edition), pages 52–63.

## Please check

1. **Application activity 4.8 (b), p. 60, is misprinted in the book.** It is printed as
   `ü/10 ÷ 1 –/4 × 2 –/3`, with digits missing, so the intended numbers are unknown.
   It is left out of Tsinda for now. If the teacher's guide or a corrected copy has the
   right numbers, tell us and we will add it.
2. **End unit assessment Q6 says "1/4 of it on school fees".** We read "it" as the
   salary, because 3/5 + 1/4 + 3/20 = 1 (the whole salary). Please confirm.
3. **End unit assessment Q4 says "inverse".** We treat it as the reciprocal (5/2).
4. **Marking schemes and model answers are written by Tsinda.** NESA does not
   publish marking schemes. Please check the marks for the free-response questions
   (EUA Q2, Q3, Q11) and the past-paper question NESA 2024 Q28.
5. **Marks for book exercises.** The book gives no marks, so each answer is worth 1 mark.

## Corrections made to the book's worked examples (in the lessons)

- 4.9 Example 1: the book writes "Kwizera got = 1 − 2/5 = 3/5"; 3/5 is what Uwera
  **kept**, not what Kwizera got. The lesson shows the correct steps.
- 4.9 Example 2: the book says "After spending on food, he remained with 1/2";
  it should be "after transport". The lesson shows the correct steps.
- Spelling: "resprocal" → "reciprocal", "symblol" → "symbol".
